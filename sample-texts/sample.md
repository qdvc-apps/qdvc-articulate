# Quarterly update for the reading group

Thank you all for joining us on monday. In order to make the most of our time, I have wrote up the main points
below. Its been a busy quarter, and their is alot to cover.

We recieved the the new books last week. The end result is that we now have a large number of titles to
choose from, which is a very good problem to have. Due to the fact that the library could of kept them
longer, we should return them prior to the end of the month.

The colour of the new covers is lovely, but the catalog lists them under a different heading. Please
organise your notes so that they are easy to find , and try and keep them short.

At the end of the day, the decision was made by the committee to meet every second Thursday. This means that
every member who has been waiting for a chance to present their favourite book will finally be able to do so
without having to wait for several months, which was the main complaint we heard in the survey last spring.
